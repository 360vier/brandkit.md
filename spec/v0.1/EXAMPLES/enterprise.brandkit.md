- short_name: Helion

- mission: Provide controlled, auditable AI workflows for regulated organizations.
- positioning: Enterprise-grade AI governance and documentation platform.

## audience
- primary_segments:
  - Platform engineering directors
  - Security and compliance teams
  - Procurement stakeholders

## voice and tone
- tone_attributes:
