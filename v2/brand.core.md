## Allowed Overrides

Subbrands and products may override:

- `Wordmark`
- `Primary Emoji`
- `Accent Color`

Subbrands and products must not override:

- `Fallback Token`
- `Portability Rules`

## Portability Rules

- Source files must remain valid Markdown.
- Use literal Unicode emoji, not shortcode syntax.
- Meaning must never depend only on emoji, color, or renderer features.
