# Voice Guidelines

Defines writing tone across brand layers.

## Core Tone

- Clear
- Direct
- Practical
- Professional

## Language Rules

- Prefer short sentences and active voice.
- Avoid vague intensifiers and marketing filler.
- Define abbreviations on first use.

## Terminology Rules

- Keep one canonical term per concept.
- Keep product names and capitalization consistent.

## Allowed Overrides

Subbrands may adjust examples and domain vocabulary.
Products may add feature-specific terms.
No layer may override clarity and readability constraints.
